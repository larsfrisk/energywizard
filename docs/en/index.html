How it works

🧠 Priority engine

A rule engine (not a guessing AI) decides in real time: heat the hot water tank, shift EV charging, store heat in the house, or back off - based on electricity price, solar surplus and demand tariffs.

🔋 The house as a heat battery

The hot water tank and the house's own thermal mass are used to "charge" when electricity is cheap and "coast" on stored heat when it's expensive - for several hours at a time.

✅ Validated against real history

New control rules are first replayed against months of real log data before going live - not just tested in theory.

🔌 Open and pluggable

Drivers for different heat pump brands, EVs and inverters - built so others can plug in their own hardware, not locked to one brand.
