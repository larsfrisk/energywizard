🌡️ The house follows the room sensors

Optional mode (Off / Shadow / On): a model trained on the house's own log picks the lowest setpoint that keeps the two coldest rooms above the floor until the next cheap period. Shadow mode only logs what it would do, so you can compare before switching it on.

💧 ML-controlled hot water

The pump waits through expensive hours when no draw is expected and refills the tank in the cheapest quarters before the next likely draw. Legionella protection, fault protection, negative prices and solar surplus always come first. Off / Shadow / On.

🚗 Charging rules per car

Goals without a ready-by time, solar only, maximum price and a solar target per car. A warning arrives straight away when the car cannot reach its goal, with how far it is expected to get.

☀️ Solar surplus order

Home battery, EV, hot tub, hot water and the house in the order you want. A load further down only starts when nobody above wants the surplus.

🛁 Bathing times for the hot tub

Pick when you usually bathe. Outside bathing times the tub cools down to the eco floor and is heated back up in time, in the cheapest hours. Run against 60 days of history: weekend bathing saved 25–34% of the tub's electricity, and the tub was hot at every bathing time. How it works.

What is actually running

Brands in the installations, from the anonymous telemetry (manufacturer and count only, never model or who). The brand comes from Home Assistant's device registry: it shows what is in the homes, not that everything is controlled.

How it works

🧠 Priority engine

A rule engine (not a guessing AI) decides in real time: heat the hot water tank, shift EV charging, store heat in the house, or back off - based on electricity price, solar surplus and demand tariffs.

🔋 The house as a heat battery

The hot water tank and the house's own thermal mass are used to "charge" when electricity is cheap and "coast" on stored heat when it's expensive - for several hours at a time.

✅ Validated against real history

New control rules are first replayed against months of real log data before going live - not just tested in theory.

🔌 Open and pluggable

Drivers for different heat pump brands, EVs and inverters - built so others can plug in their own hardware, not locked to one brand.
